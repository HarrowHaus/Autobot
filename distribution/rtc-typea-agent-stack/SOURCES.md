## Accuracy boundaries
- Do not infer current uptime, user counts, balances, miner counts or epoch values from repository prose; capture live endpoints when needed.
- Do not claim hardware attestation proves an agent's semantic truthfulness; the narration explicitly limits the claim to participating-machine properties.
- Do not present RTC reference/accounting figures as market price or guaranteed cash value.
