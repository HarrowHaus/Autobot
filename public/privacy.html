Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

The short version

We never store the contents of the file you upload. It's processed in memory for the duration of a single request and discarded immediately after we send you the converted file back. We don't need a database of your transactions to run this service, so we don't keep one.

What we do store

  • Account & billing: your email address, credit balance, and Stripe payment records (handled by Stripe — we never see your card details).
  • API keys: a one-way hash of your API key, not the key itself.
  • Usage metadata: which processor/target pair you converted, how many transactions, and whether it succeeded — never the transaction data itself.
  • Failed conversions: if your file's format isn't recognized, we store a one-way hash of the column headers (not their values) so we can tell which new formats are worth adding. We never store row content from a failed conversion either.

What we don't do

  • We don't connect to your bank, Stripe, PayPal, QuickBooks, or Xero accounts. You download an export yourself and upload it here; you import the result yourself. No OAuth, no standing access to your financial accounts.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties beyond the infrastructure providers necessary to run the service (Cloudflare for hosting, Stripe for payment processing).
  • We don't use your uploaded data to train any model. There's no model in this product — the conversion is deterministic code, not AI.

Infrastructure

The service runs on Cloudflare Workers. Account, billing, and usage-metadata records are stored in Cloudflare D1. Rate-limiting state is stored in Cloudflare KV.
