Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026

What this is

PayoutSplit converts payment-processor export files (Stripe, PayPal) into files formatted for import into QuickBooks Online or Xero. You provide a file; we return two converted files. That's the whole service.

Not accounting advice

PayoutSplit is a data-formatting tool, not an accounting, bookkeeping, or tax service. We don't verify that your source export is complete or accurate, and we don't verify your chart of accounts. You are responsible for reviewing the converted files before importing them and for confirming the result is correct for your books. If you're not sure, check with a bookkeeper or accountant.

No warranty

The service is provided "as is." We do our best to handle common export formats correctly and test against known column layouts, but processor export formats can change without notice, and we can't guarantee every file will convert correctly. If a conversion looks wrong, don't import it — let us know instead.

Billing

Conversions are pay-per-use. Your first conversion is free; after that, purchases are one-time credit packs — there are no subscriptions and no recurring charges. Credits don't expire. We don't offer refunds for used credits, but unused credits can be refunded on request within a reasonable time.
