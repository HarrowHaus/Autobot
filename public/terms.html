Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026

What this is

PayoutSplit is an early alpha. It reads a Stripe Payout Reconciliation Itemized CSV export and produces a reconciliation report — per-payout charges, refunds, fees, and any variance between the file's stated net and an independently recalculated net. It does not currently produce a QuickBooks Online or Xero import file, and it does not support PayPal or any processor other than Stripe.

Not accounting advice

PayoutSplit is a data-review tool, not an accounting, bookkeeping, or tax service. The reconciliation report is informational — it is not accounting-ready and should not be recorded to your books without review. We don't verify that your source export is complete or accurate. You are responsible for reviewing every figure before using it for anything. If you're not sure, check with a bookkeeper or accountant.

No warranty

The service is provided "as is," as an alpha. We validate strictly against one documented Stripe export format and reject anything we can't confidently interpret rather than guess — but we can't guarantee the report is complete or error-free, and Stripe's export format can change without notice. If a report looks wrong, don't rely on it — let us know instead.
